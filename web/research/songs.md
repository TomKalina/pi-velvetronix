# Song presets (punk rock focus)

`source` = URL about the gear used (or `null` = no reliable gear source, settings from style reasoning). Equipboard and forum sources are crowd-sourced; treat as secondary.

## Notes

- Off pedals carry valid, neutral params (pedal1 off = TUBE OD 30/50/50/50/50/50, pedal2 off = TAPE ECHO 350 ms, reverb off = SPRING mix 15) so switching one on is never silent or out of range.
- Drive pedal LEVEL kept around 45-55 so presets do not jump much in loudness; program volume is untouched.
- `model` and effect `type` are numeric protocol ids (protocol.md §3).
- Czech presets: no gear sources found; years for Hudba Praha and Sto zvířat are unknown (`null`). Visací zámek Traktor 1990 is assumed (debut album year), not verified.
- Bad Religion 21st Century (Digital Boy): 1994 = Stranger Than Fiction re-recording (original 1990 on Against the Grain).
